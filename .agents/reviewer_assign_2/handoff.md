## 4. Adversarial Review & Stress-Testing

### Challenge 1: The 84cm Staging Crowding Constraint
- **Challenged Assumption**: 6 adult speakers can stand adjacent to an A0 poster ($84.1\text{ cm}$ wide) during an evaluation without occluding the visual canvas.
- **Stress Scenario**: If all 6 speakers crowd in a tight line, their bodies will block the poster from the judges.
- **Assessment of Guide Defense**: Section 5.0 and 5.1 design a staggered, perimeter-based choreography:
  - Only the active speaker steps forward to point and speak.
  - Other speakers hold perimeter positions (1.0m to the left, bottom-left, top-right, bottom-right) outside the direct line of sight.
  - The final bow (Section 5.2, line 725) is the only moment all 6 step forward in a single line, which occurs after the presentation has concluded.
- **Status**: **PASS — Robust Staging Architecture**.

### Challenge 2: Zone 3 and Zone 4 Horizontal Width Density
- **Challenged Assumption**: Zone 3 (Global Expansion) and Zone 4 (Timing Pipelines) have a width of only $23.0\text{ cm}$ (compared to $39.0\text{ cm}$ for corner zones), yet contain extensive regulatory and engineering data.
- **Stress Scenario**: Handwritten text might overflow horizontally into the $20.5\text{ mm}$ gutter or Central Hub aura.
- **Assessment of Guide Defense**: The wireframes in Section 3.4 and 3.5 use vertically stacked modular cards (3 cards in Zone 3, 4 cards in Zone 4) using succinct, bulleted metric pills rather than continuous prose sentences, ensuring clean typographic containment within $23.0\text{ cm}$.
- **Status**: **PASS — Layout Capacity Verified**.

### Challenge 3: Inking and Watercolor Bleed Failure Mode
- **Challenged Assumption**: Pigment ink will withstand watercolor washes without bleeding.
- **Stress Scenario**: Inexperienced draftsmen may accidentally use standard water-based rollerball/gel pens or fail to allow adequate ink curing time.
- **Assessment of Guide Defense**: The guide explicitly mandates archival waterproof pigment liners (Sakura Pigma Micron), specifies a mandatory 20-minute curing duration before erasing pencil lines, and requires the "Wet-on-Dry" watercolor technique.
- **Adversarial Craftsmanship Advisory**: The team should mandate a 5-minute pigment swatch and water wash test on a scrap piece of 300 gsm paper before applying watercolor to the final poster sheet.
