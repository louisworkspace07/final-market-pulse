## 2. Logic Chain

1. **Strategic Product Positioning -> Global Regulatory Boundary**:
   - Market Pulse's core value proposition is **educational, objective, fact-based financial analytics** powered by Explainable AI (XAI) and Quantum simulation.
   - It does **not** execute discretionary trades or hold client custody funds.
   - Therefore, in all three target jurisdictions (Singapore, US, Japan), Market Pulse strategically qualifies for **non-discretionary information publisher safe harbors** (MAS FAA Impersonal Research Exemption, SEC *Lowe v. SEC* Publisher Exemption, Japan FSA Information Provider Safe Harbor). This dramatically reduces initial regulatory capital expenditures and compliance lead times while enabling fast market entry.

2. **Enterprise Human Capital -> Conflict Resolution Architecture**:
   - In a 6-person early-stage FinTech startup, interpersonal friction or operational negligence can paralyze product delivery.
   - Objective telemetry (Git commits, PR turnaround, uptime SLAs, peer reviews) must replace emotional arguments.
   - A multi-tier progressive resolution system ensures: (a) blameless diagnosis of root causes, (b) clear 30-day PIP accountability, (c) graceful role re-allocation to individual contributor (IC) tracks, and (d) structured legal offboarding with automated IP lockouts and reverse vesting execution.

3. **Physical Media Constraints -> Mathematical A0 Canvas Grid**:
   - An A0 watercolor canvas (841 mm x 1189 mm) viewed by evaluators from a distance of 1.5 to 3 meters requires a clear hierarchy: central focal point (Hub) and 6 radially organized thematic quadrants (Spokes).
   - Exact mathematical partitioning guarantees visual balance, zero overlap, 20 mm breathing margins, and sufficient whitespace for hand-drawn watercolor brush strokes.
   - Dedicated color coding maps cognitive domains (Blue = Tech, Green = Finance, Red = Risk, Purple = Quantum, Amber = Global Strategy, Charcoal = Text/Borders).

4. **Public Communication -> 6-Speaker Role Synchronization**:
   - Group presentations fail when transitions are clunky or roles are imbalanced.
   - Structuring speaking segments around the 5W1H core zones assigns each C-suite officer their exact domain of authority.
   - Providing 3 scalable timing models (5-minute elevator, 10-minute standard, 15-minute deep dive) guarantees operational flexibility during competitive judging.

## 3. Caveats

- **Regulatory Evolution**: Sandbox guidelines and licensing thresholds in Singapore (MAS), the US (SEC), and Japan (FSA) are subject to policy updates. While current safe harbors allow impersonal analytical publishing without full broker-dealer licensing, adding auto-trading or copy-trading features in later phases will trigger full discretionary licensing requirements.
- **Data Licensing Commercials**: Real-time market data feeds (Polygon.io, Alpaca, Quick, TDnet) carry recurring institutional API license costs that must be factored into the Series A financial model.
- **Physical Poster Medium**: Hand-drawing with watercolor pens on A0 paper requires pencil under-sketching, waterproof pigment inks, and careful drying times to prevent color bleeding between adjacent zones.

##### Tier 1: Objective Telemetry & 360-Degree Peer Feedback
- **Objective Telemetry Dashboard**: Decisions are never based on personal perceptions or hearsay. Team members are tracked via automated engineering and business telemetry:
  - *Engineering*: Sprint velocity in Linear/Jira, Pull Request review turnaround time (<24h), Git commit frequency, build break frequency.
  - *Business*: Metric milestones hit on time, regulatory documentation delivery, stakeholder response SLAs.
- **Bi-Weekly 360° Peer Evaluations**: Structured, anonymous evaluation using a 5-point Likert scale assessing: (1) Technical Reliability, (2) Communication Professionalism, (3) Emotional Stability & Temperament, (4) Cross-functional Support.
- **Trigger**: Any member scoring below 3.0 or accumulating 2 consecutive sprint delivery failures triggers Tier 2.

##### Tier 2: Blameless Retrospective & 1-on-1 Root Cause Analysis
- **Protocol**: A structured, confidential 60-minute session facilitated jointly by the CEO and the CFO/Legal officer.
- **Core Methodology**: Adapting Google's "Psychological Safety" and FAA aviation blameless post-mortem framework:
  - Separate the *human* from the *process failure*.
  - Investigate root causes: Is the team member experiencing burnout, imposter syndrome, family crisis, or unmanageable technical debt?
  - Establish a written "Mutual Recovery Agreement" with specific support mechanisms (e.g., re-scoping deliverables, offering technical assistance, mandatory 3-day wellness leave).

##### Tier 3: Formal 30-Day Progressive Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
- If negligent, uncooperative, or volatile behavior persists after Tier 2, a formal legal **30-Day PIP** is enacted:
  - **Specific Binary Milestones**: 3 to 4 non-negotiable deliverables with exact completion deadlines and automated test passing requirements.
  - **Weekly Formal Check-ins**: Documented in writing every Friday at 16:00 UTC, signed by the employee and CEO.
  - **Explicit Consequences**: The document states unambiguously that failure to meet 100% of the agreed milestones will result in role re-allocation (Tier 4) or employment termination (Tier 5).

##### Tier 4: Role Re-Allocation & Functional Decoupling
- **Scenario**: The individual possesses irreplaceable algorithmic or domain genius (e.g., highly specialized quantum mathematics) but displays chronic emotional volatility, making people management or customer interaction destructive.
- **Action**:
  - Re-assign the member from a C-suite executive role (e.g., Head of AI) to an **Individual Contributor (IC) / Principal Research Fellow** role.
  - Remove all direct reports and managerial authority; route all team communication through an intermediary product manager.
  - Decouple their research assignments from the sprint critical path, neutralizing operational bottlenecks for the rest of the company.

##### Tier 5: Phased Separation Procedure & IP Safeguards ("The Clean Break")
- If the individual fails the PIP and remains toxic or irresponsible, immediate formal termination is executed:
  1. **Founder Shareholder Agreement Enforcement**:
     - *Reverse Vesting Schedule*: All founders are bound to a standard **4-year reverse vesting schedule with a 1-year cliff**.
     - *Bad Leaver Clause*: Breach of employment contract, chronic willful neglect, or behavioral gross misconduct classifies the member as a "Bad Leaver". All unvested equity is immediately forfeited back to the company pool, and vested shares are subject to corporate clawback at par value ($0.001/share).
  2. **Automated Intellectual Property & Security Lockout**:
     - **Automated Revocation Script**: Executed simultaneously with the formal termination notice. Within 60 seconds:
       - Google Workspace SSO, Slack, and corporate email deactivated.
       - GitHub Organization access, Git commit rights, and SSH deploy keys revoked.
       - AWS IAM roles, root database access keys, and production API tokens rotated immediately.
  3. **Legal Assignment & Restrictive Covenants**:
     - *Proprietary Information and Inventions Agreement (PIIA)*: Re-verifying that 100% of all code, models, documentation, and algorithms authored belong solely to FinTuition Inc.
     - *Non-Compete & Non-Solicitation*: Enforcing non-solicitation of clients/employees and a 12-month non-compete covenant within applicable local labor law standards, accompanied by a standard separation settlement.
